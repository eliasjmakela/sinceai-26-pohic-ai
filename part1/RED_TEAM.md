# Red-team pass on the v2 weighing concept

Method: after the first draft of H1 + H2 and H5, a separate pass (same agent, deliberately adversarial, no new
design work allowed in the pass itself) tried to break each design against the brief's list (frost, wet cardboard,
biowaste, overloads, vandalism, COMBI transport) and against our own calculations. Each finding is then either
**revised** (the design or a number in `WEIGHING_CONCEPT.md` changed) or **accepted** (the risk stays, stated in
§15 Limitations and/or the validation plan). The team has no mechanical-engineering expertise, so "addressed" means
"addressed on paper"; an engineer must confirm (`ENGINEER_QUESTIONS.md`).

| # | Failure mode | Hits | What would happen | Response | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Frost and ice.** Water in the plate gap or under the rails freezes | H1, H5 | H1: plate frozen to its stops → reads ~0, or a frozen-in charge is "weighed" late. H5: ice bridges rail to ground → load bypass, under-reading | H1: perimeter seal, drain hole under the plate, and the **cycle-count jam alarm** (10 strokes without a weight change; fires within 12–78 h, `generated/fusion.md`); a frozen plate is a detected fault, not a silent error. Below the cells' −10 °C compensated range [20] the zero is re-taken every stroke anyway. H5: zero check on every exchange flags a shifted zero; winter is in pilot stage D | revised (alarm, drain) + accepted (winter performance unknown, Q7) |
| R2 | **Wet cardboard.** Rain or snow adds water mass | all | The device weighs the water, as the weighbridge does; H1 gets heavier, stickier charges (more bridging) | Not a sensor error (v1 §7). Wetter charges change the bridging share per container, which is inside the 2–5 % container-to-container term of the H1 model; H2 tracks slow seasonal change through the drift term | accepted (limitation: price on dry mass needs a separate agreement) |
| R3 | **Grit, glass or a jammed item in the 0.24–0.48 mm stop gap** | H1 | Plate rests on the stops → load bypass → under-reading or zero | Seal + wiper lip on the plate edge; differential reading makes a *constant* bypass harmless only if it is constant, so the jam alarm and the H2 ratio watch are the backstop; an elastomer layer for a larger gap was considered and rejected for now (creep and hysteresis, `calc/loads.py`) | revised (seal, alarm) + accepted (bench stage A must show it) |
| R4 | **Biowaste liquids and leachate** run into the gap; acids corrode | H1 | Seized or corroded cells; liquid mass drains away after reading B | Stainless IP68/IP69K cells [22], drain path below the plate into the container sump (*assumed* to exist, Q6); liquid that leaves the plate before reading A is counted as having gone into the container, which is where it goes | revised (cell grade, drain) + accepted (corrosion life unknown) |
| R5 | **Overload and misuse.** A 25 kg dense item thrown hard; a whole bin tipped in; someone stands on the plate | H1 | Up to ~12 t peak on a 2 t plate (`calc/loads.py`) | Overload stops are mandatory and sized for the stroke load (7–14 t) which is larger anyway; cells' safe limit 150–200 % [20] covers normal charges (a 30 kg bag peaks at ~2.5 t) | revised (stops in the design) |
| R6 | **COMBI transport and tipping.** The unit is pulled up, driven and tipped at the treatment site; contents slosh back into the chamber | H1 | Plate loaded during transport; liquid and debris over the plate | Readings are ignored while the unit is away; the plate's own inertia (~100–150 kg × a few g) is inside the stops; sum and zero restart when the unit returns. Liquid ingress = R4 | revised (reset rule) + accepted (pilot stage D counts tipping cycles) |
| R7 | **Vandalism and tampering.** Customer or third party blocks the plate or rails, cuts a cable | H1, H5 | Wrong or missing weight | Billing stays on the weighbridge, so nobody gains from tampering with the device weight; a cut cable is a sensor fault in SMART; a blocked plate trips the jam alarm; a biased H5 shows in the ticket ratio (drift watch) | accepted (low incentive) |
| R8 | **Wedged cardboard (bridging) changes from day to day** | H1 | The calibrated gain is right on average but each container differs | This is the floor of H1 after calibration (2–5 % container term, team estimate); it is why H1's σ is 2.3–5.4 % and not 1 %. Pilot stage B measures the real share | accepted (largest unknown) |
| R9 | **v1 T1 flaw found here:** strain gauges on a COMBI frame measure bending moment, which depends on where the unit stands after each set-down | T1 | Gain changes from lift to lift; v1's 1.9 % estimate was too optimistic | Added "support position" (0.5 / 2 / 4 %) to the T1 budget; T1 σ is now 1.2 / 2.8 / 5.3 % (`generated/error_budget.md`). This is one reason T1 is no longer the chosen tier | revised (numbers changed) |
| R10 | **DUO coupling carries part of the container weight** | H5 (DUO) | Load bypass: the rails see less than the container weight, varying with how it docked | Read only after the coupling is released or in a defined state; or accept a per-device gain that H2 learns; must be checked (human-review 8) | accepted, open question |
| R11 | **Hook-lift pull forces on the rails** (3.8–7.6 t horizontal on dry skids, `calc/loads.py`) | H5 | Shear-beam cells side-loaded → damage, hysteresis | Check rods / stay rods take horizontal force [23]; the cost model has a €500–1,400 mechanical line for it (team estimate) | revised (restraint in design and cost) |
| R12 | **Set-down impact on the rails** | H5 | Stiff rail + fast set-down reaches the 10 t safe limit of a 5 t cell | Overload stops under all four cells; keep 5 t cells (10 t cells double zero drift, `calc/temperature.py`) | revised |
| R13 | **Operator shares no weighbridge tickets** | H2 | No calibration for that device | Fleet prior gives 4.6 % instead of 9.5 % at mid (`generated/self_calibration.md`); annual test-weight check; in the synthetic data 78 devices are in this group | revised (fleet prior) + accepted |
| R14 | **Drift between tickets** (debris slowly building up under the plate) | H1 | Slow under-reading | Kalman random walk + drift watch (v1) + jam alarm; drift sd 0.3–1 % per month is in the simulation | accepted (rate unknown) |
| R15 | **Ram starts before reading B** (busy sites, firmware) | H1 | Missed charges: under-reading | Firmware delay of 2–3 s; missing pairs (0.5–3 %) are in the model as a bias H2 removes | revised (firmware rule) |
| R16 | **Freedom to operate.** Close patents: floating baler bed [29], ground scales under a detachable compactor container [31] | H1, H5 | Licence or redesign needed | No novelty claim; FTO search before productisation (§11) | accepted, flagged to Europress |
| R17 | **H1 retrofit cost** €1,270–4,923 is above the ~€2,000 reference (team estimate) | H1 | Installed base cannot get H1 cheaply | Recommend H1 as factory-fit; installed base gets T0 software first, H5 where a site needs accuracy | revised (rollout split) |
| R18 | **Our error model is a guess.** Every H1 σ comes from team-estimate parameters | H1, H2 | The 3.4 % could be 10 % | Low / mid / high parameter sets carried everywhere; validation stages A–C measure each parameter; all numbers tagged *estimate* | accepted |

Result: 18 failure modes; 11 led to a design or number revision, 7 are accepted risks with a test in the
validation plan or a question for Europress.

## H7 hydraulic self-lift (added 7 Nov evening; same method, applied to the team document [39] and §19)

| # | Failure mode | Hits | What would happen | Response | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R19 | **Centre of mass unknown at partial fill.** One lift gives only the moment W·x_w | H7 | With a fixed x_w the error is ≈10 % (case A, `generated/h7_budget.md`), the PDF's own half-full figure | Fill/ram-profile model of x_w, calibrated per device from shared tickets (case B, 4.4 % mid). A two-end lift solves W and x_w together (case C, 3.2 % mid), with its extra cost stated (€510–1,150). The ±3 % in [39] became an untested target | revised (model, target) + accepted (the model is untested) |
| R20 | **Seal friction, stick-slip and cold seals** | H7 | Friction is 3–17 % of the lightest tare force; asymmetric friction survives averaging; higher in winter | Slow constant-speed lift and lower (pressure-compensated throttle), plateau detection, rejection of non-flat plateaus, a hold reading at rest as a cross-check, and the friction logged as a seal-health signal; 10–30 % residue carried in the budget | revised (procedure) + accepted (winter residue, stage A) |
| R21 | **Ground eats the 10 mm stroke margin**: guide rails, plinths, soft or frozen ground | H7 | Foot does not reach the ground, or sinks: no plateau, or a reading during settling | Stroke-out detection → "no reading", never a wrong number; foot pad in the site-work line (gravel, hot asphalt need 376–435 mm square); longer stroke or a pad on the rail cross-members for rail sites (Q13) | revised (firmware, cost line) + accepted (rail sites open) |
| R22 | **Point load on a rear frame not designed for it** (31–57 kN) | H7 | Local frame damage or cracking | Frame reinforcement in the mechanical line, FEA in NRE (€150–400k total), question Q11 | revised (cost) + accepted (needs Europress) |
| R23 | **Unattended self-lift is a new hazard**: hook-lift pulls during a reading, hose bursts, door open, night noise | H7 | Injury, damage, complaints; a CE problem | Interlocks (hook attached, door open, ram running), motion sensor triggers lowering, shear pin [39], load-holding valve, relief valve caps the force, readings outside pick-up windows; risk assessment and CE file update under [48] counted in NRE | revised (design, cost) + accepted (noise; CE work not done) |
| R24 | **Pivot shift between tare and pick-up** (settling, frost heave, unit nudged) | H7 | The machine moment no longer cancels: ≈2 % at 5 cm (`calc/h7_hydraulic.py`), more than the review's 0.8 % | Tare and pick-up in the same placement; a step in the nightly trend flags a shift; tickets (H2) absorb any steady part | revised (number corrected) + accepted |
| R25 | **Tare changes on the outside**: rain water, ice, snow on the roof | H7 | Snow can add ≈0.7–1.4 t (team estimate), read as waste | Weather data flags snowfall days; a jump in the nightly trend without compaction cycles is rejected; 25–150 kg of ordinary tare change is in the budget | revised (rule) + accepted (snow days give no reading) |
| R26 | **Rod creeps out in transport** and hits the road or the truck | H7 | Damaged cylinder, unsafe transport | Spring return + position sensor; SMART allows pick-up only with the rod retracted [39] | revised (taken from [39]) |
| R27 | **H7 adds cost but no revenue** (review §3–4) and reaches only new mobile units | H7 | Europress pays €1,034–3,008 per unit for nothing it can charge for | H7 is proposed only for the reference subset that trains the virtual scale; the pipeline shows that ~5 % of devices is enough, so new COMBI units suffice | revised (role) + accepted (business case to confirm) |
| R28 | **Prior art is close**: Hiab estimates mass and centre of mass from the hooklift cylinder [42]; pressure weighing on lifts [40][41][43][44] | H7 | Licence or redesign; overstated novelty | No novelty claim; the new part is stated narrowly (a compactor lifting itself at the site, feeding fleet calibration); FTO read of [42] before productisation | accepted, flagged |

Result for H7: 10 failure modes; 9 led to a design, procedure, number or role revision (with an accepted residual in
most), 1 (R28) is accepted and flagged.
