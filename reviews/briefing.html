The idea in one breath

Customers pay per trip, and only the incumbent operator knows each site's volumes. That history is a local monopoly: challengers can't price per kilo without it. OpenWeight puts the weight in the compactor and lets every operator see it on equal terms.

Operators pay, customers get it freedecided

Why it matters: Tommi confirmed serving operators is Europress strategy, and SMART Insights reaches only ~15 % of customers. A free customer side avoids the hardest sale. Fee: €20 per device per month (decided; ≈ €2.6 per tonne in the market model).

Europress never drives a truckdecided

Why it matters: Tommi said it plainly at kickoff. Anything that sounds like transport procurement or route optimisation works against us with the Europress judge.

Billing stays on the certified weighbridgedecided

Why it matters: EU Directive 2014/31/EU needs an approved instrument for billing. Our weight is planning data and a cross-check, so cheap sensors are enough. The cross-check still gives the customer peace of mind and the operator a reason to report honestly.

The customer owns the data and consentsdecided

Why it matters: it answers "who owns the weight?" before a judge asks, and the consent step is a natural demo moment.

Customers also get visibility, not just a better pricedecided

Why it matters: tenders happen every ~3 years; contract monitoring (on-time visits, missed visits, ticket sharing, invoice vs contract) gives value every month, which Tommi asked for.

Can the hydraulic self-lift work?worth exploring

Yes, as a feasible idea to explore. The physics is standard: at rest, cylinder pressure × piston area = supported force. Hydraulic weighing is already established on hook-lift trucks and forklifts, so the principle is proven elsewhere. Its strongest argument is Europress-specific: the compactor already has a hydraulic power pack, so the added hardware is one cylinder, a valve and a pressure sensor.

What has to be solved, with the usual fixes:

  • Seal friction and hysteresis (a few % of force): read during a slow, steady lift and lower, and average the two.
  • Lifting one end only: the reading depends on where the waste sits. Use the ram's compaction profile, or lift both ends; per-device calibration absorbs a steady bias.
  • Oil temperature, ground, safety: 10 mm stroke on rails or soft ground, a new point load on the frame, CE work for a self-lifting machine.

Pitch it as an untested target with a validation plan, combined with the virtual scale and ticket calibration, so it does not need ±3 % on its own. Check prior art for compactor-side hydraulic weighing before calling it new.
